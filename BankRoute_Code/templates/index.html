HUMAN-CENTRED DECISION SUPPORT

Route banking enquiries
without guessing.

A guarded intent-routing copilot for first-line service agents. It recognises ten card-payment and ATM issues, explains the evidence, and escalates uncertainty instead of forcing a label.

INTENDED USE

Suggest a specialist queue for an English customer-service message.

EXPLICIT NON-USE

No financial advice, account decisions, authentication or transaction execution.
